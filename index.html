Daily Routine

Feeding

  • Milo: 1 can wet food + automatic feeder (once/day), 1 can + Marbles' leftovers (twice/day).
  • Izzy: ½ can wet + 3 dry nuggets + dry food (once/day), ½ can + 3 nuggets (twice/day).
  • Marbles: ½ can wet + dry food (once/day), 2.7 oz wet food (twice/day).

Food located in the the front closet. Dry food is located at the bottom of the shelf. Marbles' feeder has a pink heart sticker on it. Izzy has a blue one. If you are coming only once a day, please use a small bowl for Marbles and Izzy's wet food. Place the dry food in the automatic feeder. If you are coming twice a day, they will be using only automatic feeder. Milo gets his wet food in the puzzle plate.

Medication

Milo Inhaler 2 puffs + 8 breaths Twice‑a‑day Dispense one puff. Watch him breath 4 times. Give him another puff. Watch him breath 4 times.
Milo Predisone 1 pill Wednesday and Saturdays Wrap pill in the pill pocket. Give one pill pocket to Marbles as well.

Litter Boxes

  • Scoop once a day; dispose of full trash bag in the trash shoot by the elevator.
  • Top up litter if below preferred depth

Play & Enrichment

  • Wand toy or laser for a few minutes with each cat.
  • Puzzle feeders or treat; put toys away afterward.

House & Safety

  • Keep doors latched. Watch the front door — Marbles is curious.
  • No lilies or essential oils around the cats.
  • If you need to go to the porch, make sure the glass door is not closed all the way so the cats can have access to the catio. Also make sure the screened door is closed all the day. I don't want them to be able to get outside unsupervised.
  • Do not leave food out. Put leftovers in the fridge or compost.
  • Rinse their cans and place it in the recycling bin

Emergency Plan

  1. Call/text Daiane. If no response in 10 minutes, call emergency contact.
  2. If medical emergency, go to ER vet and notify me from there.
  3. Bring carrier, this sheet, and my authorization if needed. I have a big carrier behind the second bedroom's door and one in the closet.
